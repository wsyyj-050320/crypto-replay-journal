# Compliance Boundary

This document is a product-safety boundary, not legal advice.

## Product Category

Crypto Replay Journal should be presented as:

- Research software.
- Paper trading software.
- Trade journaling software.
- Historical replay software.
- Risk review software.

It should not be presented as:

- A trading signal service.
- A financial advisor.
- A copy trading platform.
- A get-rich-quick product.
- An automated live trading bot for end users.

## Required Disclaimer

Use this disclaimer across the app, README, reports, and launch pages:

For research and educational use only. Not financial advice. Crypto markets are risky, volatile, and can result in loss of capital. Paper trading, backtests, and historical replays do not prove future live performance.

## Naming Rules

Allowed:

- Market anomaly
- Momentum alert
- Volatility warning
- Risk score
- Watchlist
- Paper trade
- Historical replay
- Review report
- Exit analysis
- Drawdown review

Avoid:

- Buy signal
- Sell signal
- Entry signal
- Guaranteed profit
- High accuracy strategy
- Best coin to buy
- Copy my trades
- Financial advice

## Payment Platform Risk

Early monetization should avoid selling:

- Trading signals.
- Strategy subscriptions.
- Crypto investment advice.
- Copy trading.
- Managed execution.

Safer early offers:

- Software installation.
- Private deployment.
- Dashboard customization.
- Data connector implementation.
- Report template customization.
- Educational documentation.

## Public Demo Rules

Every public demo must state:

- The data is historical, simulated, or sample data.
- Fees and slippage assumptions are visible.
- Backtests and replays do not prove future results.
- Alerts are risk or market-context alerts, not trade instructions.

## Live Trading Boundary

Public MVP should not include live execution.

If live execution is ever exposed, it must be:

- Disabled by default.
- Self-hosted only.
- Protected by deterministic risk controls.
- Clearly separated from paper trading.
- Never controlled directly by AI-generated decisions.
- Never marketed as safe because paper trading looked good.

## Copy Review Checklist

Before publishing a page, check:

- Does it imply the product tells users what to buy?
- Does it imply profit, accuracy, or easy money?
- Does it hide fees, slippage, sample size, or drawdown?
- Does it confuse watchlists with recommendations?
- Does it show paper performance as if it were live performance?

If any answer is yes, rewrite the copy.
