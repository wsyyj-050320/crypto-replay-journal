## Hero Prompt Summary

Premium editorial product visual for a crypto research software product, showing a calm dark research desk, abstract dashboard interface, equity curve, candlestick replay timeline, risk journal notes, and daily report sheets.

Constraints:

- No text inside the image.
- No exchange logos.
- No coin logos.
- No buy or sell buttons.
- No performance promises.
- No hype or gambling aesthetic.

## Thumbnail Prompt Summary

Polished demo video thumbnail showing a crypto strategy replay workspace with chart replay timeline, risk journal panel, and daily report preview.

Constraints:

- No embedded text.
- No directional trade labels.
- No exchange logos.
- No coin logos.
- No profit numbers.
