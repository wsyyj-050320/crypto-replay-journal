## Reddit Post Draft

Title:

I turned my crypto trading bot into a paper trading and risk journal. Here is what I learned.

Body:

I originally built a personal crypto quant system around scanning, paper trading, reports, and live-risk controls. The live side quickly became the least useful part from a product perspective: it adds platform risk, regulatory risk, and false confidence.

So I am extracting the safer parts into an open-source tool:

- Market anomaly scanner.
- Paper trading simulator.
- Trade journal.
- Strategy replay.
- Drawdown and exit review.
- Daily HTML reports.

The product avoids directional trade calls and focuses on reviewing decisions after the fact. Fees, slippage, drawdown, and sample size are visible by default.

I am building it as a research and journaling tool, not financial advice. Happy to share the repo with anyone who wants to test the early version.
