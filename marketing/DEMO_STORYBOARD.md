# Demo Storyboard

## Goal

Create a 75-90 second demo video that sells the workflow, not a profit claim.

## Visual Assets

- Hero image: `assets/hero-dashboard.png`
- Video thumbnail: `assets/video-thumbnail.png`
- Product demo: `demo/index.html`
- Sample report: `reports/daily-review-sample.html`

## Scene 1: Open

Screen: landing page hero.

Voiceover:

> I stopped building a crypto trading bot and turned the safer parts into Crypto Replay Journal.

Overlay:

> Paper trading. Strategy replay. Risk journaling.

## Scene 2: Dashboard

Screen: product demo dashboard.

Voiceover:

> The dashboard starts with review context: anomalies, paper equity, drawdown, worst loss, and current risk level.

Overlay:

> No directional calls. No promises.

## Scene 3: Market Anomaly Scanner

Screen: scanner table.

Voiceover:

> The scanner highlights unusual market moves. It does not provide trade instructions.

Overlay:

> Anomaly, volatility, risk context.

## Scene 4: Paper Trading

Screen: paper trading section.

Voiceover:

> Every simulation includes visible fees, slippage, stop distance, and planned risk.

Overlay:

> Practice before risking real money.

## Scene 5: Trade Journal

Screen: journal section.

Voiceover:

> The journal separates strategy problems from behavior problems: FOMO, overtrading, good exits, broken rules.

Overlay:

> Was it the setup, the exit, or the behavior?

## Scene 6: Strategy Replay

Screen: replay section.

Voiceover:

> Replay compares what happened with fixed targets, trailing exits, and no-stop scenarios.

Overlay:

> Replay the decision candle by candle.

## Scene 7: Daily Report

Screen: sample report.

Voiceover:

> At the end of the day, it creates a research report for review and tomorrow's watchlist.

Overlay:

> Watchlists are not recommendations.

## Scene 8: Close

Screen: landing page boundary section.

Voiceover:

> For research and educational use only. Not financial advice.

Overlay:

> Crypto Replay Journal
