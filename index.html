Product surface

Built for review loops, not hype loops.

  1. ScanDetect unusual moves and high-volatility context.
  2. SimulatePaper trade entries, stops, targets, fees, and slippage.
  3. ReplayCompare fixed target, trailing exit, and no-stop scenarios.
  4. JournalTag FOMO, overtrade, good exits, and broken rules.
  5. ReportGenerate daily market and decision-review artifacts.
