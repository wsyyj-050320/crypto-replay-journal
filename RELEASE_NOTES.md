# Release Notes

## 0.1.0 - Product Preview

Initial public-facing product package for Crypto Replay Journal.

Includes:

- Landing page.
- Interactive product demo.
- Sample daily review report.
- Fictional sample anomaly and paper trade data.
- Product Hunt gallery images.
- Social preview image.
- Video thumbnail.
- 30-second narrated promo video.
- Subtitle file.
- Compliance boundary and publishing checklist.

Positioning:

- Research and educational use only.
- No directional calls.
- No performance promises.
- Not financial advice.

Known limitations:

- The demo uses fictional sample data.
- The current MVP is static/local-first.
- No live exchange connection is included.
- No cloud sync or multi-user storage is included.
